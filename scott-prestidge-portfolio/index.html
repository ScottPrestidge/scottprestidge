About me

I’m Scott, an architecture graduate from New Zealand, now based in Dubai. My background brings together creative research, live events and the practical demands of running busy venues.

At RMIT, I explored how AI can support architectural design while making the human decisions behind the work visible. In hospitality, I led teams, coordinated suppliers and managed the details that shape a guest’s experience. At Mt Buller, those responsibilities extended to venue-side delivery for Buller Open-Air, an M87 × Red Bull activation for around 600 guests.

I’m now looking to bring that combination into an experiential agency, supporting clients and creative teams as ideas move from a brief into a live experience.

What I bring

Live deliveryVenue planning, staff briefings, guest experience and on-the-day issue resolution.
